<h1>Global Elevation Model (GDEM) Dataset</h1>

<p>
This project gathers global digital elevation model datasets for the Earth,
so dependent projects can consume elevation and seafloor relief in verified,
common forms.
</p>

<h2>Status</h2>

<ul>
  <li>ETOPO 2022: downloaded; see the dashboard for tile counts, size, and download date.</li>
  <li>Further datasets are planned: Copernicus DEM GLO-30 and NASADEM are marked for download in the datasets manifest.</li>
  <li>Void handling: deferred until more datasets are downloaded; ETOPO 2022 has no voids by construction.</li>
  <li>Common data format: under consideration, to simplify ingestion by dependent projects.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Datasets</h2>

<p>
Datasets are stored on the dedicated datasets volume and recorded in a
manifest with id, purpose, download date, and source.
The dashboard shows the current numbers.
</p>
